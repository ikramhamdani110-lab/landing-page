Creative Technology Studio

TALORA is the area for your talent to grow

TALORA collects valuable skills across design, engineering, creative technology and strategy, then connects that talent with projects where those skills can create real value.

The Studio Ecosystem

A premium ecosystem connecting clients with skilled people.

Talent

TALORA brings together people with valuable skills across technology, design, creativity and strategy.

Project

A client brings a project to TALORA and explains what they need, what they want to achieve and what the project requires.

Match

TALORA identifies the person or team whose skills are the strongest fit for that project.

Build

The selected talent works on the project, turning the client's needs into the actual product or experience.

Deliver

The finished work is delivered to the client through TALORA.

Earn

The client pays TALORA. TALORA keeps the agreed share, and the remaining amount goes to the person or team who completed the work.

Grow

Talent gains experience and new opportunities, clients can return with new ideas, and TALORA continues connecting the right people with the right projects.
